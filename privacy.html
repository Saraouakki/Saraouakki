About the verification code

The one-time code we send you is held only in our server's temporary memory, expires after five minutes, and is erased as soon as your order is placed. We never store it alongside your order.

3. What we don't collect

It is worth being just as clear about what we never receive:

  • No card or bank details. Varisia is Cash on Delivery only. You pay the courier in cash when the parcel arrives. We have no card form, no payment processor, and no way to charge you.
  • No passwords or accounts. There is nothing to sign up for, so there is no password to lose.
  • No national ID, passport, or date of birth.
  • No advertising or analytics profiles. As of the date above, this site runs no analytics tool, no advertising pixel, and no third-party marketing tracker.
Keep this honest

The last point is true of the site as it is built today. The moment you add Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Analytics, or any ad tracker — which most stores eventually do — this section becomes false and the cookies section below has to be rewritten, most likely with a consent banner. Update this page in the same session you add the tracker, not later.

4. Why we use your information

We use what you give us for these purposes and nothing else:

  • To verify that the phone number on an order is genuine.
  • To pack, ship, and deliver your order, and to collect the cash payment on delivery.
  • To let you track your order's progress.
  • To contact you about that order — for example if an item is delayed, out of stock, or the courier cannot find your address.
  • To handle returns, exchanges, warranty claims, and support questions.
  • To keep basic business records of what we sold and to whom.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Marketing messages

We currently do not send marketing messages. If we ever start, we will ask for your agreement first and every message will include a clear way to stop receiving them. Agreeing to marketing will never be a condition of buying from us.

Have this reviewed

Rules on SMS and WhatsApp marketing are strict and vary by country, and WhatsApp's own Business Policy adds requirements on top of the law. Before sending any promotional message to a customer who bought via COD, get advice on what consent you need to capture at checkout and how to record it.

Have this reviewed — and act on it

Two things need attention here. First, name your actual couriers and suppliers: several data-protection laws require you to identify recipients, or at least the categories, rather than saying "our partners". Second, when you use a dropshipping supplier, that supplier receives your customer's home address. Ask an advisor whether you need a written data-processing agreement with each provider — with Twilio, Google, your host, your couriers, and any supplier who receives customer addresses — as this is a common requirement and is usually a standard document each provider already offers.

6. Where your data goes

Some of the providers above store or process information on servers outside the country you live in. Twilio and Google, for example, operate internationally. This means your information may be transferred across borders in the course of delivering your order.

Have this reviewed — this is the most important item on the page

Cross-border transfer of personal data is the area where the countries you sell into differ most, and it is the hardest part to get right from a template. Morocco, the UAE, and Saudi Arabia each have their own data-protection regime with its own rules — and in some cases its own registration or notification step with a national authority before you begin processing customer data or sending it abroad. Do not treat this section as adequate. Ask a professional, per country you ship to, what you must register, disclose, or put in place before transferring customer data outside that country. This is the single item most worth paying for advice on.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your order record for [NUMBER] months after delivery so we can handle returns, warranty claims, and support questions, and to meet our accounting and tax record-keeping obligations. After that we delete it or strip it of anything that identifies you.

Your one-time verification code is deleted within minutes, as described above.

Have this reviewed — and fix it in the system too

Right now the store keeps order records indefinitely, in both the server's order file and the Google Sheet, with no routine that deletes anything. Whatever retention period you write here, you need an actual process that carries it out in both places — otherwise the page states something the business does not do, which is worse than having no page. Ask your advisor what minimum retention your tax and commercial-record obligations impose, since that usually sets the floor.

8. How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe:

  • The site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • Every order requires a verified phone number, which limits fake and malicious orders.
  • Access to our order log is limited to people who need it to fulfil orders.
  • We never handle your card or bank details, because we never take card payments.

No website can promise perfect security, but if a breach ever affected your information we would tell you and the relevant authority as required.

Have this reviewed — and tighten the system

Claims in a security section should be ones you can defend. Three specifics worth raising with a technical advisor before publishing: order records are currently stored as plain, unencrypted text on the server; the order-tracking page reveals an order's product and status to anyone who has or guesses the order ID, with no second check such as the phone number; and the promise to notify people of a breach implies a plan for detecting and reporting one, which needs to exist. Ask what the breach-notification deadline is in each country you serve — several are measured in days.

  • Stop using it for anything beyond completing your order.
  • Send you a copy of the details you gave us.

Email varisia.shop@gmail.com and we will respond within [NUMBER] days. We may ask you to confirm the phone number used on the order so we do not hand your details to someone else. Exercising any of these rights is free and we will not treat you differently for it.

Have this reviewed

The rights people actually hold, and the deadline you have to respond, are set by the law of the customer's country — not by what this page offers. Confirm both the list and the response window per country, and make sure you can genuinely fulfil a deletion request across every place the data sits: the server file, the Google Sheet, your email inbox, and anything already sent to a courier or supplier.

10. Cookies and tracking

Varisia does not set advertising or analytics cookies, and does not track you across other websites.

Our pages load fonts and styling from Google and a public content network. Doing so reveals your device's IP address to those services, as it does on most websites. Your browser may also store small amounts of data locally to keep the checkout working as you move through it.

Have this reviewed

This section is accurate today and is unusually clean — worth keeping that way. Ask your advisor whether the countries you ship to require a cookie or consent banner even for this minimal setup, and revisit immediately if you add any pixel or analytics tool.
